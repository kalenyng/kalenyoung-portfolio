## Summary

A tool I use and extend: transactions roll into a daily balance, future bills and income shape a short forecast, and the UI calls attention early when the maths says trouble is coming.

## Current state

Built to track daily and forecast future cash flow. Active development — adding features, fixing edge cases, refining the forecast logic as I use it daily. Supabase + PostgreSQL handles auth and storage without overhead.

## Why it is on the site

Shows the same Supabase + TypeScript discipline as Propza, but grounded in a product I use daily. Smaller scope than a multi-tenant SaaS, heavier on date math and projections. Built around a simple constraint: you cannot spend money twice.
